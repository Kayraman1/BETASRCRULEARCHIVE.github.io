Official community ruleset

Forza Horizon 6 [2026]

Select a heading to open or close that part of the rule tree.
  • General Rules
    • All runs require a complete video for verification.
    • The video must remain permanently accessible to the Mod Team.
    • Public or unlisted YouTube videos, Twitch Highlights, and other permanent video-hosting sites are allowed.
    • Twitch VODs, private YouTube videos, TikTok videos, Xbox Clips, mega.nz links, and other temporary or file-sharing services are not allowed.
    • If the video becomes unavailable, the run will be rejected. Evidence that the video was previously available will not be accepted.
    • All runs must include in-game sound. Silent runs will be rejected.
    • The full HUD must remain enabled throughout the run.
    • The Replay feature cannot be used as a recording method. Runs recorded using Replay will be rejected.
    • The use of mods in any form during runs is strictly forbidden. This includes modifying the game or using mods to obtain additional items or currency.
    • Listing the car and tune used in the run description is recommended, but not required.
    • The Mod Team reserves the right to request details about the car and tune used.
    • If a run is declared invalid after re-evaluation, the decision is final. Further direct messages or spam concerning the decision will not be answered.
    • Track cuts are allowed. Checkpoints are the only enforced track limits.
    • All DLC and additional content are allowed unless stated otherwise in the category rules.
    • The order in which events, races, stunts, and other objectives are completed is left to the runner unless specified otherwise.
    • Additional overlays using the game’s Data Out feature are allowed if the game supports it.
    • The submitted platform must be set to PC when Data Out overlays are used.
    • All difficulty settings are allowed except Assisted Braking, Assisted Steering, Auto-Steering from Forza Horizon 5 onward, and ANNA’s Auto Drive.
    • All Kind of Runs using Seasonal Aspects will be rejected. The Exception is Showcase Remixes or Seasonal Events which you cannot opt out of.
  • Forza Horizon
    • Forza Horizon 6 [2026]
      • Full Game Runs
        • All upgrades and tunings are allowed.
        • All difficulty settings are allowed except Auto-Steering, Assisted Steering, Assisted Braking, and ANNA’s Auto Drive.
        • Pausing and opening menus count toward the final time.
        • For achievement-related categories, such as All Barn Finds, timing ends when the associated achievement requirements have been completed.
        • Timing is measured using real time (RTA).
        • For runs longer than 4 hours, the runner may take a total break of up to 1 hour. This may be taken all at once or split into multiple breaks.
        • For runs longer than 10 hours, the runner may take a total break of up to 2.5 hours.
        • Any% (Horizon)
          • Timing starts when the player first gains control of the Nissan GT-R NISMO on the ground during the Initial Drive.
          • Timing ends when the screen fades to black after completing the Gold Wristband Event.Or the Designated Wristband Color for Wristband Runs.
        • Any% (Japan)
          • Timing starts when the player first gains control of the Nissan GT-R NISMO on the ground during the Initial Drive.
          • Timing ends when the the Gold or the Respective Categories Stamp is obtained.
        • All Races / All Races [Category] / All Wristbands
          • For All Races, all Road Races, Dirt Races, Cross-Country Races, Street Races, Touge Races, and Drag Races must be completed.
          • For All Races [Category], all races belonging to the selected category must be completed.
          • For All Wristbands, all Wristband Events must be completed.
          • Resetting the game or using a new save is not required.
          • Timing starts when “Start Race” is selected for the first race.
          • Timing ends when the player crosses the finish line in the final race.
      • Individual Races
        • Track cuts are allowed. Checkpoints are the only enforced track limits.
        • The final time is the time shown by the game after completing the race.
        • The final time must be shown using an in-game screen at the end of the submitted video.
        • Custom layouts are not allowed.
        • Changing car restrictions, weather, season, and time of day is permitted for the Standard Race category.
        • For Festival Races, the settings must remain unchanged.
        • The original Forza race layout and race length must be used.
        • Rewinds are not allowed.
        • The car’s class must be visible at some point during the run for verification.
        • The car’s class must be visible at some point during the run for verification.
        • For Point to Point Rivals the runner may not drive back to build up more Speed and must start from the Starting Position.
        • For Rivals With Laps/Timeattack's the runner may only use the prior Lap to build up speed.
        • For Time Attacks/Rivals, the car’s class must be shown before or after the attempt.
        • Driftattack
          • The Runner may only use the Prior Lap to Build up Speed or Angle the car. And Rewinds are not Allowed
          • Time is Calculated by the Dirftpoints the runner accumulates during the Run. To Calculate the Time it is Recommended to use the PR Stunt Calculator on the Main Page with the "Driftzone" Setting. If the Runner wishes to Calculate the Time Themselves the Formula is 1 Drift Point=1 Milisec.
        • Wristband Events
          • Timing uses in-game time (IGT).
          • The final time is the time shown by the game after completing the Wristband Event.
          • The final time must be shown at the end of the submitted video.
