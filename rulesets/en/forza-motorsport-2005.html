Official community ruleset

Forza Motorsport [2005]

Select a heading to open or close that part of the rule tree.
  • General Rules
    • All runs require video verification.
    • Videos must be securely and permanently hosted. YouTube or permanent Twitch VODs are recommended.
    • If the video becomes unavailable, the run may be rejected.
    • Moderator decisions made after a re-evaluation must not be targeted with further direct messages or spam.
    • Game sound must be present during the run.
  • Forza Motorsport
    • Forza Motorsport [2005]
      • Full Game Runs
        • Any difficulty settings are allowed.
        • Any upgrades and tuning settings are allowed.
        • Pausing and opening menus count toward the final time.
      • All Races
        • Timing begins when the Start Race button is pressed.
        • Timing ends on the final frame where the player controls the car in the last race.
        • Any car available from the player’s garage may be used if the game permits it.
        • Timing is measured using real time (RTA).
      • Series Runs
        • Timing begins when Start Race is pressed for the first race in the selected series.
        • Timing ends on the final frame where the player controls the car in the last race of that series.
        • Timing is measured using real time (RTA).
      • Time Trials
        • Timing uses in-game time (IGT).
        • The final time is the value displayed by the game at the end of the time trial.
      • Career Runs
        • Unlock-category timing begins when the player confirms the profile name.
        • Unlock-category timing ends when the game confirms that the category has been unlocked.
        • Career Any% timing begins when the player confirms the profile name.
        • Career Any% timing ends on the final frame of the last required career race.
        • Career runs may be completed in multiple recorded parts.
          • Before pausing, show the garage, available money, and the active timer.
          • Show the same information before continuing the next recorded part.
      • Individual Level Runs (IL)
        • Timing uses in-game time (IGT).
        • The submitted time must match the result displayed at the end of the race.
